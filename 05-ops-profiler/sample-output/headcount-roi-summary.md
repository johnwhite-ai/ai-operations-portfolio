# Sample Engagement — Headcount ROI Summary

*The tool did not stop at "here is the data." It built the financial case for the system. Anonymized.*

**Scope:** B2C + Parts small-parts operation, 10-hour shift
**Purpose:** estimate the labor cost the automation would displace — the ROI basis for the proposal.

## Current-State Labor (estimated from movement data + on-site walkthroughs)

| Role | FTEs | Basis |
|------|------|-------|
| Pickers | 14 | line volume / picker rate |
| Replenishment | 3 | inbound + putaway volume |
| Packers | 6 | order volume / pack rate |
| Leads/support | 2 | span of control |
| **Total** | **25** | — |

## Labor Cost Basis

| Parameter | Value | Source |
|-----------|-------|--------|
| Avg fully-loaded wage | $22.50/hr | customer range + benchmark |
| Annual hours/FTE | 2,080 | standard |
| Annual cost/FTE | $46,800 | computed |
| Total current labor | $1.17M/yr | 25 FTE |

## Post-Automation Labor (estimated)

| Role | FTEs | Change |
|------|------|--------|
| Pick station operators | 6 | −8 |
| Replenishment | 2 | −1 |
| Packers | 6 | unchanged |
| Leads/support | 2 | unchanged |
| **Total** | **16** | **−9 FTE** |

## ROI Summary

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| FTEs displaced | 9 |
| **Annual labor savings** | **$421K** |
| Savings basis | 9 × $46,800 |

**Confidence:** Estimated. Current-state FTEs triangulated from movement data and walkthroughs; wage from customer range. Validate on site visit. Also output as an Excel ROI workbook for the deal team.
